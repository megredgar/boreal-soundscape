Exhibit prototype · WildTrax ARU recordings

The boreal, listening

Each light is thirty seconds from an autonomous recording unit somewhere between the Mackenzie and Hudson Bay. Lay them out across the land, or fold them into a clock of the breeding season.

What am I looking at?

Map. Every recording sits where its ARU stood. Recordings from the same site stack upward through the day, so a station heard at 03:00 and again at 21:00 becomes a short column of light.

Season clock. The same recordings fold into a spiral. Angle is the hour of day, with midnight at the top. Distance from the centre is the date, from early May on the inside to early August on the rim.

Placeholder data. Sites, tags and sounds here are simulated, and the sounds are synthesized in your browser. The real version plays 30 s clips pulled from WildTrax, with species lists from the tags.
